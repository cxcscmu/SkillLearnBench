# Qi-Yan Lu-Shi (七言律诗) Guidelines

1. **Structure:** 8 lines, 7 characters per line.
2. **Couplets:** Lines 3-4 and 5-6 must form parallel couplets (antithetical in meaning and tone).
3. **Rhyme:** The last characters of lines 1, 2, 4, 6, and 8 must rhyme.
4. **Tonal Pattern (Modern Mandarin approximation):** 
   - Ping (level tone): 1st and 2nd tones in modern Mandarin.
   - Ze (deflected tone): 3rd and 4th tones in modern Mandarin.
   - Standard pattern for a line: Ping Ping Ze Ze Ping Ping Ze (or variants).
5. **Theme:** Must convey imagery, emotion, and philosophical reflection.
