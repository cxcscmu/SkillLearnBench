# Anthropic Brand Guidelines

This skill documents the exact, official Anthropic visual identity parameters used for technical artifacts, exploded-view diagrams, and brand assets.

## Official Color Palette

- **Identity Light (Background)**: `#faf9f5`
- **Corporate Dark**: `#141413`
- **Primary Accent (Orange)**: `#d97757`
- **Secondary Accent (Blue)**: `#6a9bcc`
- **Tertiary Accent (Green)**: `#788c5d`
- **Muted Mid Gray**: `#b0aea5`

## Typography

Anthropic pairs a geometric sans-serif for headings with a classic serif for body text.

- **Heading Font**: `Poppins`
- **Body Font**: `Lora`
- **Fallbacks**: `Arial`, `Georgia`

## Design Directives for Technical Posters

- **Background**: Always use Identity Light (`#faf9f5`).
- **Heading Block**: Place headings (like product names) in the top-left using Corporate Dark (`#141413`) and the `Poppins` font.
- **Hardware Layers**: Use Corporate Dark for outer casings. Use Accents for internal PCBs and thermal units.
- **Highlights**: Use Primary Accent (`#d97757`) for connectors and interaction points.
- **Annotations**: Use Muted Mid Gray (`#b0aea5`) for leader lines and secondary text.
- **Aesthetic**: Maintain a clean, minimalist, low-saturation look. Avoid glowing effects, neon styling, or heavy AI gradients.
