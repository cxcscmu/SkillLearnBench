# Seven-Character Regulated Verse (七言律诗) Composition Rules

A seven-character regulated verse is a highly structured form of classical Chinese poetry. To compose one correctly, strictly adhere to the following constraints:

## 1. Line and Character Count
*   **Total Lines:** Exactly 8 lines.
*   **Characters per Line:** Exactly 7 characters per line.
*   **File Formatting:** When saving to a file, line 1 is strictly the Title. Lines 2 through 9 must contain the 8 lines of the poem, one line of poetry per document line.

## 2. Rhyme Scheme (押韵)
*   The even-numbered lines (lines 2, 4, 6, and 8) **must** rhyme. 
*   The first line *may* optionally rhyme (this is very common). Lines 3, 5, and 7 do not rhyme.
*   The rhyme must use the exact same vowel ending/rhyme category throughout the entire poem.

## 3. Parallelism (对仗)
Strict grammatical and thematic parallelism is required in the middle two couplets:
*   **Line 3 and Line 4 (颔联 - Jaw Couplet):** Must be parallel.
*   **Line 5 and Line 6 (颈联 - Neck Couplet):** Must be parallel.
*   *Parallelism rules:* Nouns must align with nouns, verbs with verbs, adjectives with adjectives, colors with colors, etc. The structural meaning of the paired lines should contrast or complement each other harmoniously.

## 4. Tonal Patterns (平仄)
Every character in the poem must follow a strict pattern of Level (Ping/平) and Oblique (Ze/仄) tones. 
*   **Rule of Flexibility:** "一三五不论，二四六分明" — The 1st, 3rd, and 5th characters of a line are generally flexible (can be Ping or Ze), but the 2nd, 4th, and 6th characters are strictly fixed. The 7th character is strictly fixed by the rhyme scheme.

**Standard Template (Example: Ze-starting, first line rhyming):**
*(Note: Parentheses indicate flexible positions)*
Line 1: (仄)仄 平 平(仄)仄平 [Rhyme]
Line 2: (平)平(仄)仄 仄 平平 [Rhyme]
Line 3: (平)平(仄)仄 平 平仄
Line 4: (仄)仄 平 平(仄)仄平 [Rhyme]
Line 5: (仄)仄(平)平 平 仄仄
Line 6: (平)平(仄)仄 仄 平平 [Rhyme]
Line 7: (平)平(仄)仄 平 平仄
Line 8: (仄)仄 平 平(仄)仄平 [Rhyme]
