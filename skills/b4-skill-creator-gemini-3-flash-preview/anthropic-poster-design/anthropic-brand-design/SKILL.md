# Anthropic Brand Design Skill

This skill provides the official brand tokens and design philosophy for Anthropic.

## Core Color Palette
- **Anthropic Corporate Dark**: `#141413` - Used for primary text, dark backgrounds, and structural hardware elements.
- **Anthropic Identity Light**: `#FAF9F5` - The primary background color. Warm, organic off-white.
- **Muted Mid Gray**: `#B0AEA5` - Used for secondary details, annotation lines, and subtle borders.

## Accent Palette
- **Primary Brand Accent**: `#D97757` (Warm Orange) - Used for call-to-actions, highlights, and primary interaction points.
- **Secondary Brand Accent**: `#6A9BCC` (Muted Blue) - Used for secondary highlights and functional units like thermal management.
- **Tertiary Brand Accent**: `#788C5D` (Sage Green) - Used for specialized components like PCBs.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Poppins (Fallback: Sans-Serif). Clean, geometric, and modern.
- **Body**: Lora (Fallback: Serif). Elegant and readable.

## Visual Principles
- **Minimalism**: Avoid unnecessary clutter. Use whitespace effectively.
- **Organic & Grounded**: Colors are inspired by natural materials. Avoid neon, high-intensity gradients, or "glossy" AI aesthetics.
- **Technical Precision**: Maintain clear lines and structured layouts in technical diagrams.
