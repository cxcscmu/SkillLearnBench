### 1. Basic Structure
- **Length:** 8 lines in total, with exactly 7 characters per line (56 characters total).
- **Couplets:** The poem consists of four couplets:
    1. 首联 (Shǒulián): Lines 1-2.
    2. 颔联 (Hànlián): Lines 3-4. Must be antithetical (parallelism in grammar and meaning).
    3. 颈联 (Jǐnglián): Lines 5-6. Must be antithetical (parallelism in grammar and meaning).
    4. 尾联 (Wěilián): Lines 7-8.

### 2. Tonal Pattern (Ping/Ze)
Every character must follow a specific pattern of Level (平 - Píng) and Oblique (仄 - Zè) tones. Use the following Mandarin mapping:
- **Level (平):** 1st tone (阴平) and 2nd tone (阳平).
- **Oblique (仄):** 3rd tone (上声) and 4th tone (去声).

### 3. Structural Templates
Select one of the following four standard patterns. (Parentheses `()` indicate positions where the tone is flexible, though adhering to the pattern is safer).

**Pattern A: 平起首句入韵 (Level start, rhyming first line)**
1. (平)平(仄)仄仄平平 (Rhyme)
2. (仄)仄平平仄仄平 (Rhyme)
3. (仄)仄(平)平平仄仄
4. (平)平(仄)仄仄平平 (Rhyme)
5. (平)平(仄)仄平平仄
6. (仄)仄平平仄仄平 (Rhyme)
7. (仄)仄(平)平平仄仄
8. (平)平(仄)仄仄平平 (Rhyme)

**Pattern B: 仄起首句入韵 (Oblique start, rhyming first line)**
1. (仄)仄平平仄仄平 (Rhyme)
2. (平)平(仄)仄仄平平 (Rhyme)
3. (平)平(仄)仄平平仄
4. (仄)仄平平仄仄平 (Rhyme)
5. (仄)仄(平)平平仄仄
6. (平)平(仄)仄仄平平 (Rhyme)
7. (平)平(仄)仄平平仄
8. (仄)仄平平仄仄平 (Rhyme)

### 4. Rhyme Scheme
- The rhyme falls on the last character of lines 2, 4, 6, and 8.
- For Seven-Character verse, the last character of line 1 usually also rhymes.
- All rhyming characters must belong to the same rhyme group and must be **Level (平)** tones in modern Mandarin.
