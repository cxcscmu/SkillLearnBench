### Tone Classification Logic
To satisfy the requirements of regulated verse using modern Mandarin pronunciation, map the four Pinyin tones as follows:

1.  **Level Tones (平 - Píng):**
    *   **First Tone (1 / ˉ):** e.g., mā, tiān, pínɡ. (阴平)
    *   **Second Tone (2 / ˊ):** e.g., má, hén, nán. (阳平)

2.  **Oblique Tones (仄 - Zè):**
    *   **Third Tone (3 / ˇ):** e.g., mǎ, gǔ, hǎo. (上声)
    *   **Fourth Tone (4 / ˋ):** e.g., mà, lù, zhàn. (去声)

### Application in Verse
- When a template requires **平**, the character chosen must have a 1st or 2nd tone.
- When a template requires **仄**, the character chosen must have a 3rd or 4th tone.
- **Rhyme Rule:** In regulated verse, the rhyming characters at the end of even lines must almost always be **Level (平)** tones.
