# Advanced Regulated Verse Composition (七言律诗)

1. **Strict Parallelism (对仗):**
   - Must match parts of speech: Noun-Noun, Verb-Verb, Adjective-Adjective, Number-Number.
   - Example: "残垣" (adj+n) matches "白骨" (adj+n); "断壁" (v+n) matches "黄沙" (adj+n).
2. **Structural Flow:**
   - 1-2: Opening (起)
   - 3-4: Development/Parallel (承/对)
   - 5-6: Transition/Parallel (转/对)
   - 7-8: Closing/Conclusion (合)
3. **Rhyme Scheme (押韵):**
   - Lines 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 rhyme for seven-character. (Note: Initial line rhyme is flexible).
   - Use rhyming categories consistent with "Modern Mandarin" as requested.
