# Modern Mandarin Rhyming (押韵)

For modern Mandarin:
1. **Groups (韵部):**
   - -an, -ian, -uan, -üan
   - -en, -in, -un, -ün
   - -ang, -iang, -uang
   - -eng, -ing, -ong, -iong
   - -ao, -iao
   - -ou, -iu
2. **Consistency:**
   - Ensure the same rhyme group is used throughout.
   - For 7-character verse, ensure end words of lines 2, 4, 6, 8 (and optionally 1) share the same group.
3. **Punctuation:**
   - Traditional punctuation: comma after 2nd/4th character, period at end of each line.
