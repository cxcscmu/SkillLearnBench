# Modern Mandarin Rhyming for Regulated Verse

## Rhyming Principles

In classical Chinese regulated verse, rhyming is based on the **final sound** (韵母 yùnmǔ) of characters. Modern Mandarin pronunciation provides clear, unambiguous rhyme determination.

### Core Concept: Finals (韵母)

The **final** of a Mandarin syllable is everything after the initial consonant. For example:
- 和 (hé): initial h- / final -e (e.g., pinyin finals: e, eh, etc.)
- 平 (píng): initial p- / final -ing
- 来 (lái): initial l- / final -ai
- 人 (rén): initial r- / final -en

### Modern Mandarin Finals System

For regulated verse, these rhyme groups work well:

#### Group 1: -ang, -ang, -ang sounds
Characters: 亡 wáng, 乡 xiāng, 常 cháng, 光 guāng, 伤 shāng, 长 cháng, 平 píng (note: píng rhymes with -ing group), 荷 hé

#### Group 2: -ing sounds
Characters: 平 píng, 鸣 míng, 声 shēng, 生 shēng, 灵 líng, 萦 yíng, 轻 qīng, 宁 níng, 京 jīng

#### Group 3: -ai, -ei sounds
Characters: 来 lái, 开 kāi, 外 wài, 悲 bēi, 杯 bēi, 泪 lèi, 微 wēi, 非 fēi

#### Group 4: -an, -en sounds
Characters: 间 jiān, 山 shān, 关 guān, 寒 hán, 残 cán, 人 rén, 深 shēn, 春 chūn, 身 shēn, 贫 pín

#### Group 5: -ong, -ung sounds
Characters: 空 kōng, 中 zhōng, 红 hóng, 风 fēng, 功 gōng, 公 gōng, 通 tōng, 崇 chóng

#### Group 6: -ao, -iao sounds
Characters: 高 gāo, 骄 jiāo, 晓 xiǎo, 翘 qiào, 照 zhào, 笑 xiào, 草 cǎo, 道 dào

#### Group 7: -uo, -o sounds
Characters: 和 hé, 多 duō, 波 bō, 歌 gē, 我 wǒ, 坐 zuò, 座 zuò, 么 me

#### Group 8: -u, -ü sounds
Characters: 无 wú, 都 dū, 出 chū, 木 mù, 绿 lǜ, 女 nǚ, 庐 lú, 书 shū

## Determining Rhyming Characters

### Step 1: Identify the Final
For each character you want to rhyme, determine its final sound in modern Mandarin:

Example:
- 和 (hé) → final: -e (or -o in some romanizations)
- 平 (píng) → final: -ing
- 来 (lái) → final: -ai

### Step 2: Match Finals
Characters rhyme if they share the same final sound:
- 来 lái, 开 kāi, 骸 hái, 害 hài all share -ai final → they rhyme
- 平 píng, 鸣 míng, 生 shēng all share -ing/-eng final → they rhyme

### Step 3: Check Pinyin Pronunciation
When unsure, refer to standard Pinyin:
- Characters with the same pinyin ending rhyme
- Example: -ang group: 亡, 伤, 乡, 常 all end in -ang → they rhyme

## Regulated Verse Rhyme Requirements

In a regulated verse (8 lines):
- **Lines 2, 4, 6, 8 must all rhyme** (same final sound)
- **Line 1 typically does NOT rhyme** with the rhyming group (though it can)
- **Line 3 and beyond**: Follow the tonal pattern while maintaining the rhyme

## Verification Process

For your regulated verse:

1. **List final sounds** for each character at the end of lines 2, 4, 6, 8
2. **Verify they match**: All should have the same final sound
3. **Double-check pronunciation**: Use standard Mandarin pinyin as reference
4. **Ensure meaningfulness**: While rhyming, characters should make semantic sense
