# Literary Emotive Writing for Poetry

## 1. Imagery of War
- **Desolation:** 蓬蒿 (penghao - weeds), 瓦砾 (wali - ruins), 杜鹃 (dujuan - cuckoo, symbolizes sad crying).
- **Violence:** 烽烟 (fengyan), 戈戟 (geji - weapons), 鼙鼓 (pigu - war drums).
- **Personal Suffering:** 鬓霜 (binshuang - graying hair), 泣露 (qilu - crying), 孤影 (guying).

## 2. Imagery of Peace
- **Agriculture:** 桑麻 (sangma), 鸡犬 (jiquan - peaceful village sounds), 丰稔 (fengren - good harvest).
- **Peaceful Life:** 太平 (taiping), 乐土 (letu), 安恬 (antian).
- **Natural Beauty:** 暖日 (nuanri), 柳风 (liufeng), 燕语 (yanyu).

## 3. Contrast (对比)
- Use contrast to highlight the suffering.
  - "Iron armors cold" vs. "Warm hearth at home".
  - "Silent ruins" vs. "Bustling markets".

## 4. Personification (拟人)
- Clouds "mourning", Rivers "weeping".
