# Comprehensive Modern Mandarin Rhyme (中华新韵)

In modern Mandarin rhyming, we focus on the final sound (vowel/ending).

## 1. Core Rhyme Groups
- **一平 (a, ia, ua):** 家, 花, 答, 瓦.
- **二波 (o, e, uo):** 河, 歌, 坐, 磨.
- **四皆 (ie, üe):** 铁, 蝶, 别, 夜.
- **五微 (ei, ui):** 归, 扉, 谁, 泪 (note: lei is oblique, but rhyme is for Level tones).
- **八寒 (an, ian, uan, üan):** 山, 烟, 关, 眠, 园, 田.
- **九十唐 (ang, iang, uang):** 乡, 霜, 望, 降.
- **十一庚 (eng, ing, ong, iong):** 风, 兵, 明, 晴, 争.

## 2. Selecting a Rhyme
- **Choose one rhyme group** and stick to it for lines 2, 4, 6, 8 (and optionally 1).
- **Mandarin Tones:** Use 1st (阴平) or 2nd (阳平) tones for rhyming lines in regulated verse.
