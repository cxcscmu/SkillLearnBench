# Qiyan Lushi (Seven-character regulated verse) Composition

This skill provides the structure and rules for composing a seven-character regulated verse in Chinese.

## Structure
- Exactly 8 lines.
- Exactly 7 characters per line.
- The poem must have a title on the first line (when outputting to a file, put the title first, followed by the 8 lines of the poem).

## Rhyme
- Even lines (lines 2, 4, 6, 8) must rhyme on the last character.
- The first line may optionally rhyme.
- When specified, rhyming should be based on modern Mandarin (Pinyin) pronunciation, meaning characters with the same final vowel sound should be used.

## Tonal Pattern (Ping-Ze)
- Follow standard Ping-Ze (level and oblique tones) rules for Qiyan Lushi.
- First and second tone in Pinyin are Ping (level). Third and fourth tones are Ze (oblique).

## Parallelism (Dui Zhang)
- The second couplet (lines 3 and 4) must be strictly parallel in grammar and meaning.
- The third couplet (lines 5 and 6) must also be strictly parallel.
