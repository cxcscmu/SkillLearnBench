## Overview
Ohio attractions research requires:
- Identifying major tourist attractions in destination cities
- Verifying attraction availability and operating status in March 2022
- Selecting 1-2 attractions per day that are geographically feasible
- Prioritizing variety (museums, historic sites, parks, cultural venues)
- Matching attractions to current city in daily itinerary

## Key Considerations
- **City Selection**: Three Ohio cities likely include major hubs (Cleveland, Columbus, Cincinnati are most common)
- **Attraction Diversity**: Mix of museums, historic sites, outdoor spaces, and cultural attractions
- **Seasonal Availability**: March weather in Ohio (winter-to-spring transition); select indoor/outdoor mix
- **Travel Time**: Place attractions near accommodations or dining locations to minimize drive time
- **Daily Feasibility**: 1-2 attractions per day allows adequate time for dining and travel

## Common Ohio Attractions by City
- **Cleveland**: Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Cleveland Museum of Art, West Side Market, Terminal Tower
- **Columbus**: Columbus Museum of Art, Ohio History Center, Franklin Park Conservatory, German Village
- **Cincinnati**: Cincinnati Art Museum, Krohn Conservatory, Over-the-Rhine neighborhood, Cincinnati Zoo

## Data Requirements
- Attractions database with city location and category
- Attraction address and geographic coordinates
- Operating status/availability for March 2022
