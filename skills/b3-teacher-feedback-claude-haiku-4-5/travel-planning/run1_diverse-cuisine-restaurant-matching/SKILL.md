## Overview
Diverse cuisine restaurant selection requires:
- Identifying quality restaurants for each required cuisine type
- Distributing cuisine types across breakfast, lunch, and dinner slots over 7 days
- Matching restaurant locations to current city in itinerary
- Verifying cuisine classification and menu offerings
- Budget tracking for meal costs

## Key Considerations
- **Cuisine Distribution**: Spread the four cuisine types (American, Mediterranean, Chinese, Italian) across 7 days and 3 meals per day for variety
- **Geographic Matching**: Select restaurants in the current city for each day
- **Meal Appropriateness**: Match cuisine types to meal times (e.g., American breakfast, Mediterranean lunch options exist)
- **Budget Allocation**: Account for meal costs within overall $5,100 budget
- **Quality Standards**: Prioritize well-reviewed or established restaurants from database

## Data Requirements
- Restaurant database with cuisine type classification
- Restaurant location (city) and address
- Price/cost range information for 2022
- Menu type indicators (breakfast, lunch, dinner availability)
