## Overview
Pet-friendly accommodation selection requires:
- Verified pet policies (dog weight/size limits, pet fees)
- Pet amenities (dog beds, food bowls, designated relief areas)
- Budget alignment per night across multiple nights
- Consistency across stay duration
- Geographic placement in or near city centers for attraction access

## Key Considerations
- **Pet Policies**: Note breed restrictions, weight limits, and daily/stay fees
- **Budget Allocation**: Calculate per-night budget (total budget ÷ number of nights) and account for pet fees
- **Amenities**: Prefer accommodations with dog-friendly features (parks, pet relief areas, nearby walking paths)
- **Location**: Choose properties near attractions and restaurants within reasonable walking/driving distance
- **Booking Verification**: Cross-reference with actual 2022 listing data

## Data Requirements
- Accommodations database with pet policy fields
- Price per night information for March 2022
- Location data (address, city, proximity to attractions)
