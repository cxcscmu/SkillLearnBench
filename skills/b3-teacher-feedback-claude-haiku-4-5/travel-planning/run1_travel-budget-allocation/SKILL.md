## Overview
Budget allocation requires:
- Breaking down total budget by expense category
- Calculating per-unit costs (nightly accommodation, per-meal, per attraction)
- Accounting for pet fees and special requirements
- Tracking cumulative costs throughout planning
- Identifying cost-saving options without compromising quality

## Budget Breakdown Example ($5,100 total)
- **Accommodations** (6 nights, pet-friendly): ~$2,400-2,700 ($400-450/night for 2 people + pet fees)
- **Meals** (21 meals over 7 days): ~$1,500-1,800 ($70-85/meal for 2 people)
- **Attractions**: ~$300-400 (assume $50-70 per day for 2 people)
- **Fuel**: ~$150-250 (estimate based on total miles and MPG)
- **Buffer/Miscellaneous**: ~$350-450

## Key Considerations
- **Daily Spending Limits**: Establish max per-day spending to track progress
- **Pet Costs**: Account for pet accommodation fees (typically $25-75/night)
- **Meal Per-Person Estimates**: $25-40 per person per meal depending on venue type
- **Free/Low-Cost Attractions**: Incorporate parks and public spaces to manage costs
- **Real Data Verification**: Cross-check estimated costs against actual 2022 pricing

## Data Requirements
- Accommodation pricing data for March 2022
- Restaurant pricing/cost ranges
- Attraction admission fees
- Fuel costs (gas prices March 2022)
