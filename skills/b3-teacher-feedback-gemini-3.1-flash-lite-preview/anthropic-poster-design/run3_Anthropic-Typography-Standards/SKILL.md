---
name: Anthropic-Typography-Standards
description: Specifies the sanctioned typefaces for Anthropic internal engineering handbooks and technical posters.
---

*   **Heading Font:** *Graphik* (or *Söhne* for specific UI-adjacent headers, per brand guidelines).
*   **Usage:** All product titles (e.g., "NOVA") must be set in the primary brand typeface, ensuring high legibility and appropriate kerning.
*   **Prohibition:** Avoid monospaced fonts (e.g., Spezia Mono) for primary headings; these are reserved strictly for code-block annotations or technical data tables.
