description: Defines the official, sanctioned Anthropic brand palette and HEX mapping for all technical documentation and hardware visualization assets.
---

The following HEX values must be applied consistently across all Anthropic design artifacts to ensure brand alignment:

*   **Background (Identity Light):** `#faf9f5`
*   **Corporate Dark:** `#191919`
*   **Primary Brand Accent:** `#d97757`
*   **Secondary Brand Accent:** `#6a9bcc`
*   **Tertiary Brand Accent:** `#788c5d`
*   **Muted Mid Gray:** `#8f8c87`

Strictly avoid high-saturation or synthetic neon deviations. Colors must maintain a low-saturation, matte-finish aesthetic.
