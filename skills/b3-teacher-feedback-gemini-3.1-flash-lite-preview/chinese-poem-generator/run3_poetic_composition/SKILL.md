To compose a seven-character regulated verse:
1. Draft exactly eight lines, each containing exactly seven Chinese characters.
2. Exclude all punctuation marks (commas, periods, etc.) within or at the end of the lines to maintain a clean character count of 7 per line.
3. Ensure the thematic progression reflects the suffering of war in the first four lines and the longing for peace in the final four lines.
4. Verify that the poem follows the required structure: title on line 1, followed by lines 2 through 9 as the poem content.
