When applying the structure for an eight-line regulated verse:
1. Map characters to the 'Ping' (level) and 'Ze' (oblique) tonal patterns across all 8 lines.
2. Select a rhyme scheme consistent with modern Mandarin phonology, applying the rhyme to the last character of lines 2, 4, 6, and 8.
3. Enforce the parallel structure (duizhang) between lines 3-4 and lines 5-6, ensuring that the semantic categories and grammatical structures of the characters in these lines correspond precisely.
