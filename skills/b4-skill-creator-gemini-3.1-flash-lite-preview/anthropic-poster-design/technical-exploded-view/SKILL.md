# Technical Exploded-View Standards

## Design Principles
- **Clarity:** Exploded views should clearly show internal component relationships.
- **Layers:** Always represent at least 5 layers in complex devices (e.g., Casing, Thermal Unit, PCB, Battery, Interface).
- **Alignment:** Use consistent vertical or exploded-perspective alignment.
- **Annotation:** Use clean leader lines (Muted Mid Gray) to point to key features.

## Layer Structure Example
1. Outer Casing (Anthropic Corporate Dark)
2. Thermal Management (Secondary Brand Accent)
3. PCB Substrate (Tertiary Brand Accent)
4. Battery Module
5. Interaction/Connector (Primary Brand Accent)

## Best Practices
- Use simple geometric shapes to represent components.
- Avoid over-detailing; focus on modularity and functional separation.
- Maintain consistent spacing between exploded parts to imply modularity.
