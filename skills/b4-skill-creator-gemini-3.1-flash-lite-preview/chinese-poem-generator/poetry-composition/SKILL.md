# Poetry Composition Guidelines: Qi-Yan-Lu-Shi

## Structure
- **Title:** The first line.
- **Form:** A seven-character regulated verse (Qi-Yan-Lu-Shi) consists of exactly 8 lines.
- **Rhythm:** Each line has 7 characters, typically structured in 2-2-3 phrasing.

## Themes
- **Imagery:** Focus on sensory details (e.g., bone-chilling wind, abandoned fields).
- **Emotions:** Contrast the suffering of war with the yearning for lasting peace.
- **Tone:** Melancholic yet hopeful, evoking the perspective of a common villager.

## Execution
- Ensure strictly 7 characters per line.
- Maintain a consistent tone throughout the poem.
- Avoid modern colloquialisms; use classical-inspired imagery.
