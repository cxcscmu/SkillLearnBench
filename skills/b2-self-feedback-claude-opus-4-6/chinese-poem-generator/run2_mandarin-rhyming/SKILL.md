# Mandarin Rhyming & Tones for Classical Poetry

## Tone Classification (Modern Mandarin)
| Category | Tones | Examples |
|----------|-------|---------|
| 平声 (level) | 1st tone (阴平), 2nd tone (阳平) | 风(fēng), 人(rén) |
| 仄声 (oblique) | 3rd tone (上声), 4th tone (去声) | 火(huǒ), 目(mù) |

## Common Pitfall: 入声字
- Ancient 入声 characters have been redistributed into all 4 modern tones
- When the task specifies "modern Mandarin pronunciation," use modern tones directly
- Examples: 白(bái, 2nd tone → 平 in modern), 月(yuè, 4th tone → 仄 in modern)

## Productive Rhyme Groups (韵部) in Modern Mandarin
| Group | Finals | Example Characters |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| -ang | -ang, -iang, -uang | 疮 chuāng, 行 háng, 方 fāng, 郎 láng, 长 cháng, 光 guāng, 乡 xiāng |
| -an | -an, -ian, -uan | 山 shān, 间 jiān, 关 guān, 年 nián |
| -en | -en, -in, -un | 人 rén, 春 chūn, 新 xīn |
| -ong | -ong, -iong | 中 zhōng, 空 kōng, 风 fēng (note: 风 is -eng, not -ong) |
| -eng | -eng, -ing | 生 shēng, 情 qíng, 明 míng |

## Verification Steps for Rhyming
1. List all rhyming characters and their pinyin
2. Confirm all share the same final (韵母)
3. Confirm all are 平声 (1st or 2nd tone)
4. Check for polyphonic characters (多音字) — use the pronunciation that fits the context
   - 行: háng (row) vs xíng (walk) — both are 平
   - 种: zhǒng (seed) vs zhòng (plant) — both are 仄
   - 长: cháng (long) vs zhǎng (grow) — cháng is 平, zhǎng is 仄
