# Composing a Peace-Themed 七言律诗

## Thematic Requirements
- **Perspective**: Ordinary person (百姓/平民) experiencing war
- **Content**: Describe war suffering, express longing for peace
- **Emotional arc**: Sorrow → Hope

## Recommended Content Structure
| Couplet | Content Focus | Techniques |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| 首联 (1-2) | Set the scene of war devastation | Vivid imagery: 烽火, 流离, 满目疮痍 |
| 颔联 (3-4) | Concrete details of suffering | Parallel contrast: abandoned farms / separated families |
| 颈联 (5-6) | Personal/human perspective | Character vignettes: old man, young wife — specific & empathetic |
| 尾联 (7-8) | Turn toward hope for peace | Rhetorical question or wish: 何当, 愿, 但愿 |

## Key Imagery & Vocabulary
- **War**: 烽火 (beacon fire), 战火, 干戈, 烽烟, 铁马
- **Suffering**: 流离 (displacement), 泪 (tears), 荒废 (desolation), 分离 (separation)
- **Ordinary people**: 百姓, 老翁, 少妇, 白发, 青丝
- **Peace**: 太平, 承平, 春风, 玉帛, 干戈化玉帛

## Literary Devices
- **借景抒情**: Express emotion through scenery (abandoned fields → sorrow)
- **以小见大**: Use small personal details to reflect large-scale war
- **对比**: Contrast past peace with present war, or war with hoped-for peace
- **用典**: Classical allusions like 干戈化玉帛, 铸剑为犁
